How to read this
  • No-show risk is the model's estimated chance that the patient will not come: low is under 15%, medium 15–30%, high 30% or more.
  • Wait is the estimated minutes past the booked time before the patient is seen. It is updated from how late you are running, so click Seen when each patient goes in.
  • A confirmed patient not yet marked as seen more than an hour after their slot is shown as overdue. Click Seen or No-show to update them.
  • New bookings arrive as Awaiting confirmation. Click Accept to confirm, or Decline or Cancel if you cannot attend (for example on leave). The patient sees your reason and is offered other doctors.
  • The estimates are saved in the predictions table each time this page loads.
